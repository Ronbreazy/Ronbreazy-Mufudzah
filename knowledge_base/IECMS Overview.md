# About IECMS

General information about the Integrated Electronic Case Management System (IECMS):
what it is, who can use it, which courts use it and how to get started.

## What is IECMS?

IECMS is the Integrated Electronic Case Management System of the Judicial Service Commission (JSC) of Zimbabwe. It is a website where court cases are handled electronically. On IECMS you can:

- file court papers online, such as summons, applications, affidavits and pleadings;
- pay court fees online;
- follow (track) your case from filing to the final decision;
- check the status of a case using its case number;
- take part in online (virtual) hearings.

The IECMS website is zimiecms.org.zw. It aims to cut delays, stop court records getting lost and make the courts more open.

## Who can use IECMS?

Who is eligible to use IECMS: IECMS can be used by members of the public, including self-actors (people going to court without a lawyer), legal practitioners (lawyers) and the police. You need your own IECMS account to file a case.

## Which courts use IECMS?

IECMS is used in the superior courts: the Constitutional Court, the Supreme Court, the High Court (General Division and Commercial Division), the Labour Court and the Administrative Court, and by the Office of the Sheriff. The superior courts no longer accept paper (hardcopy) filings, so cases there are filed on IECMS.

The Magistrates Courts are being moved onto IECMS province by province (Mashonaland Central, Matabeleland North, Masvingo, Matabeleland South, Manicaland and Mashonaland East so far, with the Midlands and Mashonaland West next). Ask the clerk of court whether your Magistrates Court already uses IECMS.

## What do I need to use IECMS?

The requirements for using IECMS. To use IECMS you need:

- a phone, tablet, laptop or computer;
- an email address;
- internet access;
- an IECMS account (see how to register).

If you do not have these, you can use an E-Filing Office at the court (see where to get help).

## How do I register, sign up or create an account on IECMS?

To register (sign up, create an account) on IECMS:

1. Open zimiecms.org.zw in your web browser.
2. Choose to sign up for a new user account.
3. Enter your name, email address, phone number and a password.
4. Follow the steps on the screen to finish registering.
5. Log in with your email address and password.

If you cannot register on your own, an E-Filing Office at the court can help you register free of charge.

## How do I log in to IECMS?

To log in (login, sign in) to IECMS:

1. Open zimiecms.org.zw in your web browser.
2. Enter the email address and password you registered with.
3. Click Login.

After you log in, the first page is Pending My Action, which shows what is waiting for you. If you are not active for a while IECMS logs you out; type your password in the Quick Login box to carry on.

## Where can I get help with IECMS if I have no computer or internet?

Help (helpdesk) for IECMS: the Judicial Service Commission runs E-Filing Offices (IECMS help desks) at court buildings. They have computers and staff who can help you register on IECMS and file your papers, free of charge. Go early: the help desks can be busy. You can also ask the court registry (the Registrar's office) or the Legal Aid Directorate for help.

## How do I pay court fees or stamp duty on IECMS?

Court fees are paid on IECMS. When the Registrar accepts and registers a new case, IECMS may create the stamp duty (court fee) for you to pay. Open the case in My Workspace to see what has to be paid, and pay it before the case can go further. If you are not sure how much to pay, ask the court registry or an E-Filing Office.

## How do I track my case or check the status of my case on IECMS?

To track your case and check its status on IECMS:

1. Log in and open My Workspace.
2. Find your case using the filter (search by case number, court or party).
3. Click the case number to open it.
4. The progress bar at the top shows what stage the case has reached. Point at a step to see what was done, when and by whom.
5. The boxes below show the affidavits, applications, pleadings and other documents in the case, and whether they are draft, finalised or ongoing.

Check Pending My Action as well, to see anything that is waiting for you to do.

## Do I have to use IECMS? Is it compulsory? Can I still file on paper?

Using IECMS is compulsory (mandatory) in the superior courts. In the superior courts (the High Court, Supreme Court, Constitutional Court, Labour Court and Administrative Court) filing is done on IECMS, and the registry no longer accepts paper filings. If you cannot use IECMS yourself, go to the E-Filing Office at the court, where staff help you file electronically free of charge.

## How do I join a virtual hearing (online court session) on IECMS?

To join a virtual hearing (a virtual court session, or online hearing) on IECMS:

1. Log in at zimiecms.org.zw on a phone, tablet, laptop or computer with internet. Register first if you have no account.
2. Open your notifications. The court sends the hearing notice with the link to the virtual hearing there, and to your email. You can also find it in Pending My Action or in the case.
3. At the time of the hearing, open the notification and click the hearing link.
4. Join a few minutes early, from a quiet place, with your case number ready.

Only people involved in the case can join. The hearing runs on the court's own virtual hearing system, not on WhatsApp or social media.

## Can a witness, or someone with no computer, join a virtual hearing?

A witness or anyone else who is not a party to the case must ask the Registrar of the court for access to the virtual hearing before the hearing date, and still needs an IECMS account.

If you have no device or internet, go to the nearest JSC court, E-Filing Office or free internet hub (for example at the Bulawayo High Court), where staff help you join the virtual hearing. In Harare, ask at the Harare High Court or the Harare Magistrates Court.
