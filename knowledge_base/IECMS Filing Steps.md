# IECMS Filing Steps

Step-by-step instructions for finding a case and filing documents on the
Integrated Electronic Case Management System (IECMS).

## How do I create a new case on IECMS? (file a new case, part 1)

To file a case (start a brand-new case or claim) on IECMS, you create a new case and then file it. A summons, or an application that starts a new case, is filed this way, as a document of the new case:

1. Log in to IECMS. You must be logged in to file a case.
2. At the top of the screen click Create New and choose Case. A blank case form opens.
3. In General Information fill in Court Level, Court, Court Registry, Case Category Type, Case Category, Cause of Action / Charge and Case Summary. Fields marked * must be filled in.
4. Use the Checklist on the form to see which information and attachments the court needs.
5. In Participants, add every party to the case (for example the plaintiff and defendant, or applicant and respondent) and any lawyer representing them.

Continue with part 2 to add documents and submit the new case.

## How do I submit a new case on IECMS? (file a new case, part 2)

After filling in the new case form, add the documents and submit the case:

6. In Other Relevant Information or Case Notes, add any extra notes about the case.
7. In Documents, click Add and attach each supporting document: give it a title and description, upload the document and its signed copy.
8. Click Save & Close. IECMS gives the case a draft number and adds it to your workspace.
9. Open the case, click Actions and choose Submit.
10. The Registrar checks the case against the court rules. If it is accepted it is registered and given a case number, and stamp duty may be created for you to pay.

## How do I find or search for my case on IECMS?

To search for and find a case on IECMS:

1. Open the menu (the three lines at the top left) and click My Workspace. The Cases list opens.
2. Use the tabs at the top (All, Amicably Resolved, Consolidated and more) to show cases in a particular state.
3. Click the filter (funnel) icon. In the Filter panel you can search by Case Number, Date Updated, Court Level, Court (for example High Court Harare), Case Category, Submission Date, Registration Date or Party.
4. To show more cases on one page, change Items per page from 10 to 25.
5. Click the case number to open the case.

## How do I open my case and see the documents in it?

To open a case and see what has been filed in it so far:

1. Find the case in My Workspace and click its case number. The case overview opens.
2. The progress bar at the top shows the stage the case is at. Point at a step to see what was done, when, and by whom (for example Registered / Case Number Allocated by the High Court Registrar).
3. Below the case details are boxes for Affidavits, Applications, Pleadings, Miscellaneous Documents/Letters and Documents, with how many are in draft, finalised or ongoing.
4. To file something in the case, click the case menu icon beside the case number. It lists Overview, General Information, Affidavits, Sheriff Requests, Applications, Pleadings, Miscellaneous Documents/Letters and Documents.

## How do I file an affidavit in my case on IECMS?

To file an affidavit in a case that is already on IECMS:

1. Open the case, click the case menu icon and choose Affidavits.
2. Click Create. In Select Type choose Affidavit (or Affidavit of Service by a Litigant in Person, or Certificate of Service), then click Create.
3. On the affidavit form choose the Party making the affidavit and the Affidavit Date, and add any Details.
4. Under Affidavit Documents click Add to attach the affidavit.
5. Under Affidavit Signed Copy click Add and upload the signed copy. This is required before you can submit.
6. Click Save & Close. The affidavit gets a number (for example AF000000001) and is saved as a Draft.
7. Click Actions, then Submit.

## How do I file an affidavit of service as a litigant in person?

To file an affidavit of service, which proves that you served (delivered) court papers on the other side:

1. Open the case, click the case menu icon and choose Affidavits, then Create.
2. In Select Type choose Affidavit of Service by a Litigant in Person and click Create.
3. Fill in Party, Affidavit Date and Related Document Type (the document you served).
4. Fill in Persons Document Served On, the Date/Time Document Served and the Delivery Method.
5. Fill in the Sworn Date, the Location and the name of the Commissioner of Oaths.
6. Attach the affidavit under Affidavit Documents and its signed copy under Affidavit Signed Copy.
7. Click Save & Close, then Actions and Submit.

## Why does IECMS say "Workflow action has not been done, because of validation errors"?

This message appears when you click Actions and Submit but something required is missing. Look on the form for text in red. The most common cause is "Signed Copy is not attached": the document was saved without its signed copy. Click Edit, add the signed copy under Signed Copy, click Save & Close, then click Actions and Submit again. Until it is submitted the document stays a Draft.

## How do I file a sheriff request to serve documents on IECMS?

To file a sheriff request, asking the sheriff to serve (deliver) court papers:

1. Open the case, click the case menu icon and choose Sheriff Requests, then Create.
2. In Select Type choose the type (Court Process, Writ, Arrest Pillar Order or Court Order to Found Jurisdiction) and a Sub-type (for example Civil Appeal), then click Create.
3. Choose the Sheriff Office / Station (for example Harare Sheriff Station), the Request Date and the date it is To be Delivered by.
4. In Sheriff Request Parties choose who the papers are for, and add the Request Details, such as the address for service.
5. Under Documents click Add and attach the papers to be served; add the signed copy.
6. Click Save & Close. It is saved as a draft (for example DRAFT-SH18/26), pending submission to the Sheriff Office.
7. Click Actions, then Submit.

## How do I file a pleading such as a notice of set-down or heads of argument?

To file a pleading, for example a notice of set-down, heads of argument, notice of opposition or notice of withdrawal (to withdraw your case):

1. Open the case, click the case menu icon and choose Pleadings, then Create.
2. In Select Type choose the pleading: Heads of Argument, Notice of Opposition, Notice of Withdrawal, Consolidated Index, Notice of Filing, Masters Report, Notice of Set-down or Consolidated Record. Click Create.
3. Choose the Parties and the Pleading Date, and add any Details.
4. Under Pleading Documents click Add to attach the pleading.
5. Under Pleading Signed Copy click Add, give it a title, upload the signed copy and click Add.
6. Click Save & Close. The pleading gets a number (for example PL000000003) and is saved as a Draft.
7. Click Actions, then Submit.

## How do I file an application in an existing case on IECMS?

To file a court application or a chamber application in a case that is already on IECMS:

1. Open the case, click the case menu icon and choose Applications, then Create.
2. In Select Type choose Court Application or Chamber Application.
3. In Select Sub-type choose the kind of application, for example Application for Joinder of Parties or High Court Application. Click Create.
4. Choose the Parties and the Application Date.
5. Under Application Documents click Add to attach the application.
6. Under Application Signed Copy click Add and upload the signed copy.
7. Click Save & Close, then Actions and Submit.

## How do I file a letter to the registrar on IECMS?

To file a letter to the registrar, or another letter, in your case:

1. Open the case, click the case menu icon and choose Miscellaneous Documents/Letters, then Create.
2. In Select Type choose Letter to Registrar. Other types include Notice of Renunciation of Agency by a Legal Practitioner and Other. Click Create.
3. Choose the Party and the Letter Date, and write the Details.
4. Under Documents click Add to attach the letter, and add its signed copy.
5. Click Save & Close, then Actions and Submit.

## How do I submit a document I saved as a draft on IECMS?

Saving with Save & Close only creates a Draft; the court does not receive it yet. Open the document (from the case menu, for example Affidavits or Pleadings, then click its number). Click Actions and choose Submit. If IECMS shows a validation error, fix what is shown in red, usually a missing signed copy, and submit again.

## What does Pending My Action show on IECMS?

Pending My Action is the first page after logging in. It has a box for Cases, Applications, Affidavits, Pleadings, Summons, Sheriff Requests, Miscellaneous Documents/Letters, Letters/Notices to Sheriff and Sheriff Payment. Each box shows how many are in Draft, how many are Pending my reaction (waiting for you to act) and, for cases, how many are Overdue.

## Why did IECMS ask for my password again?

Your session expired. IECMS logs you out after you have not been active for a while, as a security precaution. A Quick Login box appears saying "To continue, first verify it's you". Type your password and click Login, and you carry on where you left off; what you typed in the form is kept. If it is not your account, click Logout.

## What does the Leave Form warning mean on IECMS?

If you start filling in a form and then click to go to another page, IECMS asks "You have unsaved changes on the form. Are you sure you want to leave this form?" Click Cancel to stay and save your work with Save or Save & Close. Click Yes only if you want to throw away what you typed.
